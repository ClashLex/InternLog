    Before you begin

    A few
    good questions.

    Can I browse without an account?

    Yes. Published internships and role details are public. A student account is needed to save a role or record an application.

    Is it free for students?

    Yes. Application tracking, saved roles, follow-up reminders, and CSV exports are free for students.

    What if I applied through another website?

    Add a manual application with the company, role, dates, status, and your notes. It stays alongside your board applications.

    How do opportunities get reviewed?

    An administrator verifies each company account. Internship posts are reviewed before publication. Unpublished and expired roles aren't included on the public board.

    Who can see my application records?

    You can see your own records. Companies see applications sent to their posts. Administrators can review application records, accounts, and posts as part of platform administration.

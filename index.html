A few useful answers

Before you
get started.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse published internships and view their details here. A student account is required to save or apply.

Can I track applications from other websites?

Yes. Add a manual application in your student workspace with its company, role, dates, status and notes.

When will a company’s internship appear here?

After the company is verified and an administrator approves the internship. Expired or unpublished roles are not shown.

Where do I see replies and follow-ups?

Your student dashboard and application tracker show recorded statuses, recent activity and follow-up reminders.

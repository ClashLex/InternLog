    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    The short version of how InternLog works.

    Do I need an account to browse internships?

    No. The board shows published roles from verified companies to everyone. A free student account is only needed to save roles or record applications.

    Is InternLog free for students?

    Yes. Tracking, follow-up reminders, saved searches, and CSV exports cost nothing.

    How do company posts get published?

    Companies register, an administrator verifies the account, and each post is approved before it appears publicly. Expired or rejected posts never show.

    Can I track applications from other websites?

    Yes. Add a manual entry with the company, role, dates, and status — it lives alongside board applications in the same pipeline.

    Who sees my application data?

    You can view your own records. Companies see only applications sent to their posts. Administrators can review application records, accounts, and posts as part of platform administration.
